# MOST Studio — UX, copy and SEO review, 22 September 2026

## Scope and evidence

Reviewed production homepage and contact page in a desktop browser; reviewed the existing mobile layout in a 390px iframe. Read the shared contact flow, editorial/service views, navigation, metadata, structured data and automated checks. Mobile iframe review is a responsive-layout check, not a physical iPhone/Safari test. This document contains no private Search Console data.

## Findings and changes

1. **Repeated work gallery on the homepage.** The hero showcase and the next section repeat the same three concepts. Retain the problem/solution/expected-benefit portfolio and its working demo links; remove the extra hero gallery. Preserve the approved arch background, headline, offer and brand palette. This makes services and contact reachable with less scrolling.
2. **Long opening explanation.** Shorten BS and DE hero copy to state the deliverable, visitor benefit, markets and online collaboration. Keep two primary actions. No fabricated results or conversion claims.
3. **Service enquiry hidden behind an extra disclosure.** On the three service pages, show the form directly and carry the selected service into it. Keep direct Instagram and Facebook access. Guides retain the optional detailed form.
4. **Generic form guidance and opaque validation.** Add persistent, service-specific instructions in both languages. Keep two required answers and optional business details. Missing/whitespace answers produce explicit inline errors and move keyboard focus to the first missing answer. Editing a prepared message remains supported.
5. **Step change can leave the heading above the viewport.** After reviewing/editing an enquiry, focus and reveal the form heading only when it is outside the usable viewport. No scrolling on initial load or ordinary option changes.
6. **Shop service proof was harder to find.** Link directly from its introduction to the working demo shop. The label explicitly identifies it as a demo.

## Research and how it was used

- [Nielsen Norman Group — Website Forms Usability](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/web-form-design/): keep forms short, explain required fields and provide specific errors. Applied to the enquiry flow; no conversion percentage is claimed for MOST.
- [Edward Sturm — simplifying conversion pages](https://www.instagram.com/edward.builds/reel/DdfKNgwvRKO/): inspected the visible video examples and accompanying caption. Applied the principle of plain language and fewer unnecessary steps, not the numerical results from unrelated experiments.
- [Edward Sturm — content refresh, intent and internal links](https://www.instagram.com/edward.builds/reel/DdiK21fPHL2/): inspected video frames and caption. Existing service pages already answer buying questions and link relevant examples. Improved the homepage introduction and shop demo path. Did not copy promises of results in days or use invented business figures.
- [Edward Sturm — Pareto SEO](https://edwardsturm.com/articles/pareto-seo/): used service/search-intent focus as a prioritization idea. Did not adopt claims that technical metadata does not matter.
- [r/agency — international GEO discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/agency/comments/1wees2a/international_geo_is_turning_into_headache/): anecdotal reminder to evaluate local buying intent rather than mechanically translating English queries. Both BS and DE enquiry guidance were written for their actual language context. Not evidence of Google's ranking algorithm.
- [r/agency — defining a new studio](https://www.reddit.com/r/agency/comments/1w7ff2q/first_year_running_a_studio_how_did_you_define/): qualitative context on clear positioning and real proof; no invented niche, team or client track record was introduced.
- [SitePoint — autofocus discussion](https://www.sitepoint.com/community/t/autofocus-attribute/489838): considered unexpected focus as a UX risk. Focus moves only after explicit form actions, not on page entry.
- [Google Search Central — AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): ordinary SEO fundamentals apply; special AI markup is not required. Kept crawlable server-rendered content and truthful structured data. No added city doorway pages, fake ratings, or ranking promises.
- [Clay](https://clay.global/): comparator for clear service grouping and proof attached to project work. Retained MOST's own design and original copy.

Reviewed selected relevant Instagram posts and their captions, not the entire account or every video. Community comments are ideas to evaluate, not validated technical instructions.

## Validation

Local lint, TypeScript/build, interaction regressions and HTML SEO checks pass. Interaction coverage includes both languages and all three preselected services, whitespace validation, focus, edited-message retention, clipboard success/failure, correct social links and explicit unsent state. Existing menu, furniture, booking, advisory and 50-product shop regression checks pass.

All 28 generated indexable pages pass self-canonical, reciprocal BS/DE/x-default, initial-HTML verification, Open Graph, structured-data, heading and crawlable-link checks. Fictional demo noindex remains intentional. Visual preview/production verification is recorded separately at deployment time.

## Remaining evidence limits

No real-user conversion test, physical Safari test, field Core Web Vitals or new private GSC measurement was performed in this review. Design improvements cannot prove rankings, AI citations or sales. The next business-proof step is an actual client case study with permission and measured results, not additional fictitious demos.
