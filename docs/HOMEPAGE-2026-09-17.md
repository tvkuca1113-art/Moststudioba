# Homepage: business value and inquiry flow

## Decisions
- Retain the established hero headline, reduce it to two primary actions.
- Replace the three-demo hero gallery with one static, real-component preview.
- Present selected work as problem / solution / intended benefit, explicitly not measured client outcomes.
- Display three cases together on desktop and an explicit three-button selector on mobile.
- Add a concise Why MOST section before the webshop.
- Move the webshop below the case studies; use a compact two-product preview on the homepage and the full four-product preview on the portfolio route.
- Condense service and process summaries. Detailed competence list is available in an accessible native disclosure.
- Add an inline project inquiry on the homepage and use the same form on the contact route, in BS and DE.

## Contact boundary
Only Instagram is a confirmed studio destination. The form prepares a message for the visitor to copy and send on Instagram. It does not claim to submit an inquiry. Email, WhatsApp, automatic form delivery, client testimonials and measured results require actual owner-provided details/evidence. No fictional contact information or proof was added. Analytics never receives typed inquiry data.
